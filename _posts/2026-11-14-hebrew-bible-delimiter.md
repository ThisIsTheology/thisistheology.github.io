The Masoretes have provided us with the Masoretic text of the Hebrew Bible, that include accents. Those accents can provide helpful information on the structure of the text, on how it is to be subdivided in smaller sections, the colons. Thomas Renz gives a good introduction in<a href = "https://www.academia.edu/26055094/Colometry_and_Accentuation_in_Hebrew_Prophetic_Poetry">this article</a>. 

I have made a little app that delimits Hebrew text according to this methodology of delimitation criticism. You can find the app <a href ="https://thisistheology.github.io/hebrew-delimiter/">here</a>.
